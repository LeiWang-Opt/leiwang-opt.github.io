About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Applied Mathematics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working with Prof. Xiaojun Chen.

I received my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Xin Liu. Prior to joining PolyU, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Statistics at Pennsylvania State University, hosted by Prof. Le Bao and Prof. Runze Li.

My research lies at the intersection of mathematical optimization and machine learning, with the goal of developing reliable and scalable algorithms for real-world applications. I study structured nonconvex optimization, distributed computation, and learning under data uncertainty.
